BEFORE YOU PLAN

A few useful answers.

Do you supply crackers for weddings and functions?

Contact Sai Baba Crackers Centre about product requirements for marriages, receptions and family functions. Availability depends on stock, your date and applicable rules. Product supply does not include operating a professional fireworks display.

Can you deliver to my area in Hyderabad?

Delivery area and timing must be confirmed by the shop. Call with your locality and preferred date before planning around a delivery. The current catalogue offers free delivery where the shop confirms serviceability.

Can I enquire outside the Diwali season?

Yes, call to discuss your occasion. Stock and opening hours can be seasonal, so please confirm availability before visiting or preparing a requirement.

How far in advance should I contact the shop?

Contact us as early as you can, particularly for weddings, larger quantities or festival dates. We do not promise a fixed lead time; the shop will confirm what can be arranged.

Does submitting a request confirm my order?

No. The catalogue records a request and prepares a WhatsApp message that you must send yourself. Final prices, stock and fulfilment must be confirmed by the shop. No payment is taken on this website. You can also call instead of using WhatsApp.

Are catalogue quantities individual pieces or packs?

Quantities refer to the selling pack listed beside each product, not individual pieces. Check the box or packet description before selecting a quantity.
